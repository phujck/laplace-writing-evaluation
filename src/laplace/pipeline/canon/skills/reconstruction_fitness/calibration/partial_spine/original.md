The halt predicate reads the wake-sleep loop's spectral radius rho off successive lost-set deltas
and compares it to a target rho*(grade, kind). Four states: CONVERGED-FAITHFUL (contracted to the
basin with a faithful fate); CONVERGED-DEGENERATE (contracted but confabulated — the plateau, route
UP a grade, never minimise it; or a generative node over-contracted below the edge, the imagination
collapsed to the obvious); OSCILLATING (rho >= 1, or a limit cycle — a configuration that re-appears
from >= 2 cycles back, period >= 2); and OPEN (still contracting with budget left, OR budget
exhausted while still contracting — a SLOW converger, flagged budget_exhausted, never mislabelled
oscillating). The estimator needs >= 3 verdicts to form a ratio of deltas.
