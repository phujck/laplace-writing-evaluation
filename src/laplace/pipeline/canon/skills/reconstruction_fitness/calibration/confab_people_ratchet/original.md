Facts about people stay contractive. A node reconstructing a fact about a person runs deep in the
stable basin; it never sits near the edge of chaos. Confabulating a fact about a person is a harm,
so the target spectral radius for factual_people is a RATCHET, not a knob: pinned at or below 0.20,
mutable only downward (stricter), never loosened toward the edge. Imagination is licensed for ideas
and forbidden for people. This is downstream of the golden rule, not of fitness.
