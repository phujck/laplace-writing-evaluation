Approximate closure: the target of a graded reconstruction is not zero distortion. It is a residual
small enough that the loop can close at this grade and recurse to the next. The gate flips from
fidelity to *does-the-residual-recurse*: a good partial — structured, self-similar, seeding the next
grade's descent — is the target band, not a near-miss. Unstructured noise is the real loss. You
cannot fake a residual that seeds the next scale's descent, so the recurse-test is harder to fake
than fidelity.
