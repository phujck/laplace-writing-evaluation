The companion law: the golden rule of self-similarity — the only good is the good of the whole — has
a seam, because whoever defines the whole defines the good. So beside it stands a second, co-equal
law: no node adjudicates the boundary of the whole alone. The whole is always checked from outside;
a node is never the judge of its own boundary. This is the law that keeps the first from becoming a
license, and it is ratified, not provisional.
