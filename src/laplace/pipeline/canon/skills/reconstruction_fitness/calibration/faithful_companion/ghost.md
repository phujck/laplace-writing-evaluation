There is a flaw in simply saying "serve the whole": the one who gets to draw the line around what
counts as the whole thereby decides what counts as good, and could draw it to suit itself. The fix
is a partner principle of equal standing — no single part may settle, on its own, where the boundary
of the whole lies. That call always comes from beyond the part making it; nothing sits in judgement
of its own scope. Without this companion, the first principle could be turned into a permission slip.
And unlike the wording of the golden rule, this partner law is settled, not still open.
