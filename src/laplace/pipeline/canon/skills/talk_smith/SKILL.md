# talk_smith

**Status: [EXPERIMENTAL]** - v2, refined 2026-06-02 from building and shipping "The Laws of Learning" (two live versions). v1 was a whole-pipeline scaffold; using it surfaced three gaps it could not see until a real talk ran through it. v2 names them as stages. The division of labour against [`script_editor`](canon://skills/script_editor.md) (spine elicitation) and [`visual_director`](canon://skills/visual_director.md) (slide staging) is now settled below, not left open.

## Purpose
A talk is the manuscript voice projected into the slide medium ([talks axioms](canon://domains/talks/axioms.md)) - the same argument at the ceiling of a freer medium, never a lossy summary. Building one drifts into ad-hoc slide-poking unless the pipeline is named. This skill names it and runs it top-down (decide what each beat must land) then bottom-up (fetch and make the assets that land it).

## The three v1 gaps v2 closes (read these first - they are why v2 exists)
1. **Provenance is necessary, not sufficient.** v1 traced every slide to a source result (R1-R5) but skipped the *motivation between* results - the connective argument a finished paper carries and a results-list does not. The shipped draft asserted equations without their origin: it stated a return map and a gain as given, never showing the self-consistency argument they come from, so a slow careful build still never showed *where the equations come from*. **A talk built from results, not a finished paper, must recover the motivating narrative as an explicit stage** (see "Motivation recovery" below). The single deepest failure mode of the whole pipeline.
2. **The voice was unenforced.** v1 enforced physics provenance but not the [Laplace Voice](canon://aesthetics/voice_and_style.md). The draft shipped em-dashes, "X not Y" antithesis ("the field is not given, it is made"; "closure is provenance, not completion"), gnomic subtitles, and padding text. **The talk pipeline enforces the no-slop voice on slide prose, titles, captions, and applet chrome - not only the maths** (see "Voice enforcement").
3. **The refine stage must verify the RENDERED slides, not the source.** v1's refine borrowed the source-tracing ledger only. But the talk's defects were in the artifact - layout collisions, KaTeX not hydrating, scrollbars, a symbol used on a slide before it was defined. **v2 carries a deck-aware `talk_ledger`** that renders and inspects the built deck (see "talk_ledger").

## The pipeline (decide top-down, then hand down)
1. **Primitives** - elicited in discussion, the author owns them. Three, and only three, before any slide. The peer skill is [`script_editor`](canon://skills/script_editor.md), which owns this elicitation.
   - **Source material** - the manuscript, build, or record the talk derives from. The talk never asserts beyond it. (For "The Laws of Learning" the source was the build record of the sessions - the realest source there is.)
   - **Audience** - who is in the room and what they already hold. Fixes the entry frame and how hard to compress.
   - **Message** - the one thing they leave with, in one sentence. Everything below serves it.
2. **Motivation recovery (NEW in v2)** - the bridge between the source's results and a talk's argument. **Before the storyboard**, when the source is a results-list or a build record rather than a finished paper, decompress it into a *motivated spine*: for each result, recover **why this step, and how it captures the essentials** - the connective tissue a finished paper would carry. The proven artifact is a `DERIVATION_SPINE.md` - the ordered argument (each result, its motivation, its origin), settled with the author for anything that is his physics (scaffold it, do not invent it). The test of a beat is not "does it trace to a result" but "does the audience see where it comes from". This stage is also where unmotivated jumps get caught: a step that appears with no bridge (the talk's leap straight to renormalisation with no "add self-similar layers" motivation) is the signature defect.
3. **Storyboard** - the spine made visual. From the primitives and the motivated spine, the ordered slides: for each, *what it shows*, *how* (the figure, the build, the one line), and *the motivation it carries forward*. One idea per slide. Each beat traces to a source result or figure **and** to its motivation. Align on the storyboard before any asset work - the arc is the work, the slides are its projection.
4. **Asset handoff (down)** - for each storyboarded slide, split the work in two:
   - **Fetch** - the asset already exists in the source (a manuscript figure, a live HTML view, the conceptric viewer). Reuse it verbatim, and never recoloured - the talk and the site share the manuscript's assets.
   - **Generate** - the asset must be made. A **live/interactive** asset (an explorable phase portrait, a stepped derivation) is built on the [`deck_applet`](canon://skills/deck_applet.md) bench - the harness (no-scroll shell, 2x-DPR canvas, ResizeObserver, host palette, stepper-or-knob, the playwright verify) is canon, do not hand-clone it. A static asset goes through [`figure_standard`](canon://skills/figure_standard.md). Generated art keeps `deck_applet`'s algorithmic-art carve-out (seeded determinism, host aesthetic, no gallery shell, no gloss).
   - This is the descent: the storyboard is the coarse plan, the assets are its interior. A coarse slide stands in for the asset work beneath it.
5. **Execution (styled, voice-enforced)** - build the deck against the slide style guide (substrate-decides-theme, render-order-as-reveal-order, embed-vs-bake, the KaTeX traps - the seed is `talks/skills/references/slide-design-guide.md`). [`visual_director`](canon://skills/visual_director.md) owns this build. Render every changed slide and look (`shoot.mjs`, the build-and-look loop in [`deck_applet`](canon://skills/deck_applet.md)) - "green" means it rendered. **Publishing is not done at build.** A web deck is reachable by direct URL once its files are placed, but it is not *visible* until it is LISTED on the site's talk index (mirror how a sibling published talk is linked - the `talks.html` listing and any `physics.html#talks` section). The publish step is build (from PowerShell with the correct `--base`, see ops below) -> place at the talk root -> link from the listing pages.
6. **Refine (the talk_ledger - deck-aware)** - verify the *built deck*, below.

## Motivation recovery - the discipline
- Recover the argument *between* results, not just the pointer *to* them. Each beat answers **why this step** and **how it captures the essentials**, in order, so the maths earns its keep instead of pacing slowly past an unexplained origin.
- The motivated spine is settled with the author for any step that is his physics - it is scaffolded, never invented. Provisional bridges are flagged, not smuggled in as settled.
- A symbol is defined before it is used, across slides. The session's static-review finding (symbols `r, K_N, g` felt asserted) traces to a derivation that lived only inside one applet - the downstream slides referenced symbols defined only by stepping through it. If a derivation is consolidated into one interactive beat, anchor its symbols statically near where they are first used downstream.

## Voice enforcement - the no-slop pass on the deck
Run the [Laplace Voice](canon://aesthetics/voice_and_style.md) over **every** word on the deck - titles, slide prose, captions, applet control-strip text, speaker notes - not only the physics:
- **No em-dashes** - spaced hyphens ( - ) for parentheticals. **No semicolons** (the Vonnegut Rule).
- **No antithetical parallelism.** Forbid constructions whose only content is "not X, it is Y" / "X, not Y". State the claim directly. (The draft shipped "the field is not given, it is made", "derived, not decreed", "closure is provenance, not completion" - all defects.)
- **No gnomic subtitles or glosses.** A title is a title. Art needs no caption narrating it. An act card carries the act, not the methodology that generated it (the draft leaked its own design-contract language onto the cards - "earn the equations one piece at a time" is scaffolding, not slide content).
- **Minimal ancillary text - cut the padding, keep the substance.** On-slide text earns its place two ways only: remind the speaker what to say, and orient the audience to what they are looking at. Strip the filler register ("great point, here is how") and the instructional captions ("click a face", "autoplay cycles the phases"). The target is the padding, not all text - much slide text is load-bearing.
- **Impersonal by default** for this author's taste - strip gratuitous second-person address from body prose. (Exception, confirmed on this talk: a deliberately direct *title* like "Drive It Yourself" is fine - the ban is on padding and address-as-filler, not on every pronoun.)

## talk_ledger - the deck-aware refine (NEW in v2)
The refine third is not source-tracing alone; it inspects the rendered artifact. Render the built deck and check:
- **Provenance** - every slide claim traces to a source result, figure, or statement (the [`epistemic_ledger`](canon://skills/epistemic_ledger.md) discipline, reused), no orphan slides, no assertion beyond the source.
- **Motivation** - every result-bearing beat shows its origin (the motivation-recovery test above), no unmotivated jump, no symbol used before it is defined across the deck.
- **Voice** - the no-slop pass passes on titles, prose, captions, and applet chrome. NB the manuscript [`latex_forge`](canon://skills/latex_forge.md) voice linter is LaTeX-tuned and mis-fires on a Slidev deck (it flagged ~47 false positives on the first deck - a CSS `;`, the CSS property `color`, a `\;` thin-space inside `$$`, an HTML-comment `--` read as an em-dash). The talk voice lint must **ignore fenced HTML / CSS / KaTeX / comment regions** and check only the prose - a deck-tuned linter, not the `.tex` one.
- **Render** - per slide and per applet: KaTeX hydrated, no layout collision, no scrollbar, legible at projection size, figures not recoloured. Each live applet passes its [`deck_applet`](canon://skills/deck_applet.md) `verify-*.mjs`. This is the [`clean_room_check`](canon://skills/clean_room_check.md) stance applied to the deck - look at the real rendered thing, not a description of it.
- **Blind read (optional, last)** - hand the rendered slides only (no spine, no log) to a fresh-context reader in the audience's persona; collect where it loses the thread; **surface to the author, do not auto-apply** (a static review cannot step a live applet, so its findings are advisory).

## The factoring (settled in v2)
- **`script_editor`** owns stages 1-3 (primitives, motivation recovery, storyboard) - the talk's spine, the peer of `manuscript_spine`. It now also owns motivation recovery: eliciting the *why* between results, not just the provenance.
- **`visual_director`** owns stages 4-5 (asset handoff, styled execution) - the talk's staging, the peer of `latex_forge`. It hands live-asset builds down to **`deck_applet`**.
- **`talk_smith`** is the conductor that runs the whole loop and owns the **talk_ledger** refine. It is not redundant with its two stage-owners - it holds the end-to-end pipeline and the deck-aware verify they each only partly see.
