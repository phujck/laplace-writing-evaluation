# voice_descent

**Status: [EXPERIMENTAL]** - forged at the thirty-first close from the voice wall: two
manuscripts passed every mechanical gate (numerals, citations, register, slop list) and were
rejected by the human judges on sight. The failure was structural, not effortful. The voice
had been applied as prose descriptions of the author's eleven distilled generators
(`soul_corpus/voice/`), and a description of a generator does not generate - any more than
a description of a Hamiltonian evolves a state.

## The law
Voice is mechanical in the generative representation only. Applying it in the lexical
representation (specs, banned-word lists, trait summaries read before writing) bends the
substrate's statistics without replacing them, and a reader detects the substrate through
every gate. The application must be vectorial and concurrent with generation.

## The protocol (retrieve-then-write)
1. **Fire the voice lens before the first sentence.** On any drafting goal whose output a
   person will read, the FIRST reconstruction call is the voice lens: `soul_corpus/voice/`
   (the distilled moves) AND the author's own prose at paragraph grade. Never reach for an
   engine-written artefact as the exemplar - the engine's welds are the substrate wearing
   instructions (the v9 mistake, named).
2. **Retrieve per passage, not per document.** For each passage about to be written, pull
   the author's nearest moves (embed the passage's intent, retrieve his closest paragraphs
   by cosine) INTO the drafting context as live exemplars. The drafter writes FROM the
   retrieved prose - imitation at the moment of generation, not recollection of a spec.
3. **Vary by the corpus, not by rule.** The multiplicity comes from retrieving DIFFERENT
   exemplars per passage. If two passages retrieved the same move, force the second-nearest.
4. **Judge against the held-out human.** A voice iteration is scored by a human verdict the
   drafter cannot pre-answer (Sarah is the standing judge; his reaction is the gate). Passing
   mechanical gates is the floor, never the verdict. Bank every verdict beside the artifact.
5. **Know the ceiling.** Retrieval narrows the gap; it cannot close it while the underlying
   generator is another model (the substrate bottleneck, his diagnosis). The closure path is
   the open-weight voice generator trained on his prose (N46) - HIS fork on spend and timing.
   This skill is the strongest move available BELOW that fork.

## When to run
Any artefact meant for a human reader's ear: manuscripts, talks, public replies, the
website. Not for internal ledgers, code, or canon (where clarity outranks voice).
