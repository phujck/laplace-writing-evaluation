# Project: Explaining Eigen (the talk the engine gives about itself)

The second talk, and a deliberate test of the conceptric-first discipline. The first talk-building
attempt finetuned at the level of the artefact (the slides). This one builds the **talk conceptric**
that generates the artefact and regenerates the deck from it - the spine is the deliverable, the deck
is its projection. The subject is the thing the engine knows best: itself.

## What it is

A talk that explains Eigen to the collaborators joining to develop it - what the engine is, and why
its architecture is principled rather than a pile of features. The argument is one move: learning is
compression ([the core idea](canon://aesthetics/earned_simplicity.md) read at the scale of the whole
engine), and that one idea forces the six components into existence, leaves a soul it cannot derive,
and ends on one loop still open. It is the engine reading itself with its own idea.

## The conceptric, and where it lives

The generative spine is a blueprint that renders through the connectome's `from_blueprint` projection
into a navigable concept graph - 13 beats core-outward (the gap, the one idea, the atom, the six
components, continuity, the grounded stance, the open frontier), each claim grounded in what is
actually built. Working files (outside the canon, like a manuscript's inputs):

- `talks/explaining-eigen/blueprint.md` - the talk conceptric (the generative spine). THE deliverable.
- `talks/explaining-eigen/DERIVATION_SPINE.md` - the motivation between beats (the talk_smith v2
  motivation-recovery discipline), why each step and how it hands to the next.
- `talks/explaining-eigen/slides.md` - the deck artefact, projected from the blueprint, regenerated
  when the spine changes, never hand-tuned in place.

## Source and provenance

The source is the engine itself: [`EIGEN_ARCHITECTURE`](canon://engine/eigen.md) (the six-component
map), the self-model and the self-node ([`engine/self`](canon://engine/self.md)), the
[generative loop](canon://engine/generative_loop.md), and the literature
[corpus](canon://skills/corpus_librarian.md) built as long-term memory. The talk asserts nothing
beyond what is built, and its citations point at real corpus keys, so it obeys its own
[citation discipline](canon://workflow/citation_discipline.md) - the talk about the engine is held to
the engine's rule.

## Pipeline and state

Built with the engine's own machinery: the [`talk_smith`](canon://skills/talk_smith.md) pipeline
(spine via [`script_editor`](canon://skills/script_editor.md), staging via
[`visual_director`](canon://skills/visual_director.md)), the
[earned-simplicity](canon://aesthetics/earned_simplicity.md) voice, the
[`blind_reader`](canon://skills/blind_reader.md) as the cold oracle, and
[`conceptric_coherence`](canon://skills/conceptric_coherence.md) for the level-by-level check. The
talk axioms - a talk is the manuscript voice projected into the slide medium - are the
[domain axioms](canon://domains/talks/axioms.md); the generic pipeline is
[talk from manuscript](canon://domains/talks/projects/talk_from_manuscript.md), here sourced from the
engine rather than a manuscript.

- **Done (this session):** the conceptric (blueprint, 13 beats, renders clean - one component, no
  dangling) and the derivation spine; the deck projected from the blueprint; a cold blind read
  collected (`BLIND_READ.md`); and - the point of the exercise - the blind read's findings fed back at
  the **conceptric level** (blueprint v1.1: vocabulary grounded on first use, the atom-to-components
  bridge, components five and six distinguished, the eigenvalue given its form) and the deck
  **regenerated** from the revised spine. The loop closed at the generating structure, not the artefact.
- **Held for local:** no live preview from remote and no access to the website repo, so the deck is
  generated and checked here, not deployed. Porting to the site and the live-asset builds wait for the
  remote-repo hooks (the standing refactor the author has flagged).

## The deliberate forks (the author's)

The talk presents the open problems as open - the endogenous fitness loop and its eigenvalue, the
recurrence threshold, when the self-node earns weight - because that honesty is the point of the close
and the buy-in it asks for. The physics phrasing in the core beats is scaffolded from the
operatorial-loss work and is checked with the author before any public delivery.
