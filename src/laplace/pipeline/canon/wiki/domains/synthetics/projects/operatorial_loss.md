**Thesis**: variational quantum machine learning optimises a single matrix element of an operator-valued loss `L(theta) = U^dagger O U`, so the barren plateau is a property of that rank-one projection, not of the landscape. The variance of the scalar gradient equals the conserved Hilbert-Schmidt norm of the operator gradient divided by the Hilbert-space dimension - the exponential decay is the denominator, not the operator.
**Scope commitment**: the contribution is the reframing (R1), the projection-artifact variance law (R2, established analytically and numerically), and the restated frontier (R5). The operator-norm-to-variance connection itself is prior art (Lie-algebraic theories); the novelty is the rank-one-projection reading and the descent prescription.
