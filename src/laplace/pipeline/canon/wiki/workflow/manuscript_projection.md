# The Manuscript-Projection Conceptric — the FORM a projector demands

A manuscript is projected from **two** conceptrics, not one. The **content** conceptric is the
substrate of results (the physics, the v7 object). This node is the other one: the **form**
conceptric — the persistent, content-free object that encodes *what a manuscript demands of any
content that wants to become a manuscript*. A [projector](canon://skills/orchestrated_generation.md)
takes `form × content` and projects the artefact from both, exactly the way the
[build conceptric](canon://skills/conceptric_coherence.md) takes a substrate × a blueprint.

This is the engine's scattered form-knowledge unified into one re-openable object. The rules
it gathers already live in the canon — in
[`manuscript_spine`](canon://skills/manuscript_spine.md),
[`orchestrated_generation`](canon://skills/orchestrated_generation.md),
[`blind_reader`](canon://skills/blind_reader.md),
[`result_foundry`](canon://skills/result_foundry.md),
[`figure_standard`](canon://skills/figure_standard.md), and the workflow nodes
[`core_outward_trunk`](canon://workflow/core_outward_trunk.md),
[`object_not_artefact`](canon://workflow/object_not_artefact.md),
[`citation_discipline`](canon://workflow/citation_discipline.md),
[`evidence_schema_flow`](canon://workflow/evidence_schema_flow.md) — but they are *dispersed*. This
node does not duplicate them; it is the **coarse node that stands in for that subgraph**, naming the
form as a single object so a projector can read it whole and so an unmet demand has a typed home.

It is itself an instance of the engine's [loop as the universal move](canon://engine/eigen.md): it
opens the outer loop, descends, closes the inner loops needed to close the loop of its purpose, and
locks. *Any good projection is self-similar to the architecture.*

## The coupling interface — form × content → manuscript

The projector is the function. The form supplies the **demands**; the content supplies the
**substance**; the projection is where they meet, and the **friction** where they do not.

- **What the form demands OF a content node.** For each form-shell below, the form names a slot the
  content must fill: a headline thesis (for the abstract ring), a motivated section with a result
  at a known status (for each inner loop), a real corpus citekey for every literature reliance, a
  real or registered asset for every figure, a derivation/numerics face for every locked claim. The
  form does **not** supply any of this substance — it supplies the *requirement* and the *shape of
  an acceptable answer*.
- **How the projection runs.** The projector walks the form's shells in the
  [core-outward reading order](canon://workflow/core_outward_trunk.md) (the framing rings last),
  and for each form-demand it pulls the content node that fills it
  ([`evidence_schema_flow`](canon://workflow/evidence_schema_flow.md): one object, many faces). A
  demand met becomes prose; the meta-instruction is **expressed through the form, never stated in
  the content** ([`object_not_artefact`](canon://workflow/object_not_artefact.md)).
- **How an unmet demand becomes a typed friction report.** When the form demands what the content
  cannot supply, the projector emits a **typed friction** under the shared
  [generation discipline](canon://workflow/generation_discipline.md) §2 — it does **not
  confabulate**, and every friction carries the CONTENT / FORM / TENSION attribution that node
  owns. The projection-specific reading of the three types: **CONTENT** is a form-slot that is
  well-posed but the content node is absent or thin (the missing `fig:keyhole` asset; a claim with
  no result; a naked reliance with no corpus record) — repaired *into the content conceptric*;
  **FORM** is a demand wrong or over-specified for this content (a genre slot the work legitimately
  lacks) — repaired *into this form object*; **TENSION** is form and content each coherent yet
  disagreeing (the hub-figure's `[POSTULATE]` label vs the transition's `established` tier) —
  surfaced, never silently resolved. The friction-row form, the no-confabulation rule, the
  CONTENT/FORM/TENSION taxonomy, and the sew-up-before-return obligation are stated once in the
  shared discipline; here they wear the form-vs-content clothes.
- **The discipline that makes the friction trustworthy: out-of-loop assessment.** Builder ≠ judge,
  the shared discipline's §2(c): the projector *builds* the manuscript; whether the manuscript is
  *good* is judged from **outside** the projector's context
  ([`generation_discipline`](canon://workflow/generation_discipline.md),
  [`orchestrated_generation`](canon://skills/orchestrated_generation.md),
  [`blind_reader`](canon://skills/blind_reader.md): a reader who shares your context cannot find the
  gap your context hides). The friction report is the projector's honest account of its own loop
  closing or leaking — *"I couldn't close my loop because of what's missing in yours"*. The
  [coherence audit](canon://skills/conceptric_coherence.md) is the projector's *internal* gate
  (is each form-shell satisfied by what fills it), **never** the judge of quality.

## The top rings — the four global laws a projection must close

These are the form's apex shells. Every manuscript-form demand below decomposes one of them. They
generalise the closure report's ten demands and the scattered canon rules into form-shells. The
first three rings (completeness / consistency / clarity) face the **content** — what the prose
says and whether it holds together. The fourth ring faces the **artefact's physical form** — the
house format, figure style, voice mechanics, and compile path the manuscript is *projected in*. A
projection can close all of content and still fail the house: a complete, consistent, clear paper
that comes out in plain `article` class with generic figures has not been projected, only typeset.

### Ring I — COMPLETENESS (every promised loop is closed)

*The introduction promised a roadmap of descent; the body must close every loop it opened, and
nothing it did not.* Decomposed into the demands:

- **D-roadmap.** The introduction supplies motivation and framing for the project as a whole and
  sets the roadmap of descent — it opens the outer loop and names, in order, the inner loops the
  body will close (the v7 intro's `Section~\ref` chain).
  Owner intuition: [`manuscript_spine`](canon://skills/manuscript_spine.md) (the macro frame
  elicited to constrain everything below), [`core_outward_trunk`](canon://workflow/core_outward_trunk.md)
  (framing rings drafted last because they summarise commitments that only then exist).
- **D-sections-close-the-primitives.** Each body section is an **inner loop** that closes one of
  the primitives the introduction promised — no promised primitive left unclosed, no section that
  closes a loop the introduction never opened.
- **D-every-claim-has-a-result.** Every claim traces to a result at a known status; a claim with no
  result is a stub, not a claim ([`result_foundry`](canon://skills/result_foundry.md),
  [`manuscript_spine`](canon://skills/manuscript_spine.md)).
- **D-figure-resolves.** Every figure resolves to a **real asset or a registered TODO** — never a
  phantom panel (the shared [`generation_discipline`](canon://workflow/generation_discipline.md)
  §1(b), pointing at [`figure_standard`](canon://skills/figure_standard.md); sharpened to
  out-of-band by Ring IV's D-asset-existence-out-of-loop). A demanded asset that does not exist is a
  CONTENT-attributed friction (the closure report's one MISSING item).
- **D-ends-on-the-frontier.** The conclusion ends honestly on the open frontier — the loops that do
  **not** yet close are named as the frontier, not smoothed; an unclosed loop *leaks upward* as the
  worklist rather than being faked closed.

### Ring II — CONSISTENCY (no naked claims; one voice; honest status)

*Whatever the work talks about is grounded — in an external reference or an internal
section/equation pointer — and the same epistemic register holds end to end.* Decomposed into:

- **D-no-naked-claims** (the weave). The microscopic self-consistency law: every claim is grounded
  either in an **external reference** (a real `\citep`) or an **internal pointer** (a `\ref` to a
  section/equation/result), by active referral back to the underlying conceptric substrate,
  references, and stored results. A claim grounded in neither is a *naked claim* — the defect this
  ring exists to close.
- **D-real-corpus-citation.** Every literature reliance surfaces as a real lowercase corpus
  citekey resolving in the bibliography — the reliance and the citation travel together, or the
  work does not pass ([`citation_discipline`](canon://workflow/citation_discipline.md);
  [`orchestrated_generation`](canon://skills/orchestrated_generation.md)'s corpus gate, which owns
  this generation-specific rule). A hand-typed key is a naked reliance.
- **D-honest-status.** Every claim sits at its honest epistemic tier — established / postulate /
  open — and the tiers are never laundered ([`result_foundry`](canon://skills/result_foundry.md)'s
  status discipline; the closure report's "three tiers, never laundered").
- **D-equation-grounded.** Every display equation is introduced and every symbol grounded on first
  use ([`evidence_schema_flow`](canon://workflow/evidence_schema_flow.md): the glossary face).
- **D-caption-true.** A figure caption claims only what its panel shows — the theory→data→caption
  loop closed on the rendered figure ([`blind_reader`](canon://skills/blind_reader.md),
  [`figure_standard`](canon://skills/figure_standard.md)).
- **D-one-document.** The whole work reads as one document through the seams of independently
  composed faces — rendered then read as one document
  ([`evidence_schema_flow`](canon://workflow/evidence_schema_flow.md)'s render-then-read loop).
  Register continuity is discharged at the weld under the versioned voice profile
  (`profiles/voice/`), which reaches only the final prose realiser and the weld
  (the 2026-07-09 rebuild mandate); no upstream stage carries voice instructions.

### Ring III — CLARITY (a stranger can follow the page)

*A reader who was not in the room can follow the manuscript from the page alone.* Decomposed into:

- **D-motivated-sections.** Every section can be given a one-line "we look at X because it reveals
  Y", and hands to its neighbour ([`blind_reader`](canon://skills/blind_reader.md),
  [`manuscript_spine`](canon://skills/manuscript_spine.md)'s `intent`).
- **D-define-before-use.** Every term and notation is defined or cited before it is used — the
  first-use census ([`blind_reader`](canon://skills/blind_reader.md)).
- **D-no-leaked-meta.** No internal label, build-rule, or operational instruction surfaces in the
  rendered prose ([`object_not_artefact`](canon://workflow/object_not_artefact.md): the leak is the
  defect; the closure report's D10).
- **D-reader-floor.** The reader's assumed floor is stated (postgraduate, numerate stranger,
  derive-don't-assert), so "you should have defined this" has a well-defined boundary — owned
  structurally by the section contracts' reader entry states
  ([`blind_reader`](canon://skills/blind_reader.md)). Register itself is not a per-section
  demand: it is discharged at the final leg under the versioned voice profile
  (the 2026-07-09 rebuild mandate).

Clarity is checked from **outside** by a context-starved reader; the projector that built the page
shares the context that hides the gap and structurally cannot judge its own clarity. This ring's
verdict is therefore always the blind reader's, never the projector's.

### Ring IV — PRESENTATION / FORMAT (the artefact wears the house)

*The first three rings make the content true, grounded, and legible; this one makes the artefact
**the house's**. A manuscript is not just words that hold together — it is a physical object with a
document class, a figure style, a voice, and a compile path, and the engine has standing standards
for all four. This is the form's appearance axis: distinct from completeness/consistency/clarity
(which are content-facing), it demands that the projected artefact carry the house mark.* This is
the **fourth global law** the form object's own fork #2 anticipated, named and placed here as a
peer ring. Unlike the content rings, Ring IV's demands are not satisfied by prose the projector
writes; they are satisfied by **pointing at the owning standard** — the form supplies the demand
and a `canon://` edge to the rule that defines an acceptable answer, and the standard owns the
detail. Decomposed into the demands:

- **D-house-document-class.** The manuscript is projected in the **house LaTeX format** — REVTeX4-2
  / APS (`\documentclass[aps,...,twocolumn]{revtex4-2}`, the two-column preprint geometry the
  column widths assume) — **not** plain `article` with hand-set `geometry` margins. The house class
  and its standard geometry are the default; departing from it is a deliberate FORM choice, never a
  silent fallback to `article`. (Owner: [`latex_forge`](canon://skills/latex_forge.md) the McCaul
  Protocol, [`compilation_and_release`](canon://operations/compilation_and_release.md). *Inferred
  default* — the canon names REVTeX4-2/APS in the real `manuscript/main.tex` preamble and assumes
  it through [`figure_standard`](canon://skills/figure_standard.md)'s exact APS column widths and
  "CM serif via usetex for REVTeX", but does not state a single class axiom in prose; encoded here
  as the house default and flagged inferred — see the fork for the author.)
- **D-figures-to-house-style.** Every figure asset is built to the house figure style — the
  **asset-conformance half of the shared [generation discipline](canon://workflow/generation_discipline.md)
  §1(a)**, here demanded *of the rendered artefact*. The shared node owns the demand and points at
  [`figure_standard`](canon://skills/figure_standard.md) (with the axiom-level appearance sources
  [`visual_mechanics`](canon://aesthetics/visual_mechanics.md) and
  [`tikz_guidelines`](canon://aesthetics/tikz_guidelines.md)) for the detail — despined inward
  ticks, exact APS column widths, viridis/Okabe-Ito, vector usetex CM serif, version-controlled
  script. Ring IV adds only that the demand binds the *projected artefact*: a generic chart with
  default aesthetics is typeset, not housed.
- **D-house-typography-mechanics.** The mechanical typesetting axioms hold: Nature-style TikZ
  colours, no reserved-keyword collisions, no layout distortions — and the draft is **linted**
  through the Forge before it is presented, not eyeballed. (Owner:
  [`latex_forge`](canon://skills/latex_forge.md) the McCaul Protocol.) Prose-register mechanics
  (spelling standard, slop vocabulary) are no longer a lint demand here: they live in the
  versioned voice profile at the final leg, guarded at the boundary by the failure-pattern
  detector (the 2026-07-09 rebuild mandate §11.5).
- **D-forge-compiler.** The artefact is compiled through the **Forge Compiler** chain
  (`compile_pdf.py` / `compile_orchestra.py`, the automated `pdflatex → bibtex → pdflatex ×2` loop),
  **never** raw `pdflatex` / `latexmk` / `bibtex` in the terminal. The compile path is part of the
  house, not an afterthought. (Owner: [`latex_forge`](canon://skills/latex_forge.md) the Forge
  Compiler Axiom, [`compilation_and_release`](canon://operations/compilation_and_release.md).)
- **D-asset-existence-out-of-loop.** Asset **existence** is an **out-of-loop** check — the
  **existence half of the shared [generation discipline](canon://workflow/generation_discipline.md)
  §1(b)**, here applied to the rendered manuscript. The builder never trusts a content node's
  `Real asset:` claim; a missing or unverifiable asset resolves to a **prose fallback** or a
  **registered out-of-band TODO**, never a rendered `\fbox` / `[FIGURE TODO]` box in the body. Ring
  IV's artefact-facing sharpening of the shared rule: a rendered TODO box is a leaked
  meta-instruction (Ring III, D-no-leaked-meta) wearing a figure's clothes, and this is what
  *resolves to a real asset or a registered TODO* (Ring I, D-figure-resolves) means — the TODO is
  registered **out of band**, not painted into the page. (Shared owner:
  [`generation_discipline`](canon://workflow/generation_discipline.md) §1(b), pointing at
  [`figure_standard`](canon://skills/figure_standard.md). *Doubly earned* — the self-supporting
  reconstruction test predicted this gap, and the blind global judge independently caught its
  symptom: the rendered `[FIGURE TODO]` box in `manuscript_v8/sections/setup.tex`. Both are recorded
  in `earned_by`.)

Ring IV **points** at the house standards; it does not copy them. The standards own the detail (the
exact column widths, the slop word-list, the compile chain); the form object owns the *demand that
they be met* and the edge that surfaces the rule at projection time. Wiring these edges makes the
house **surface** the next time a projector reads the form — it does not, by itself, re-project any
manuscript already on disk; bringing v8 (or any standing artefact) up to the house is a separate
downstream re-projection step.

## The self-similar loop architecture (the design law — the author, verbatim)

> *Any good projection is self-similar to your architecture. It opens the outer loop and descends,
> until it has closed the loops needed to close the loop of its purpose. Specifically: the
> introduction supplies the motivation and framing for the project as a whole, setting the roadmap
> of descent. Then the sections that follow are each the inner loops necessary to close as the
> primitives the introduction is promising. Then within sections, appendices are used for the
> granular detail that truly binds the work to the corpus of the outside world. The self
> consistency across the whole work is maintained at the microscopic level by active checking and
> referral back to the underlying conceptric substrate, references and stored results. It weaves
> until the finished product has no naked claims, where whatever it talks about is grounded either
> in an external reference or a manuscript section/equation pointer.*

The form encodes this as four nested moves, each a loop in the
[universal sense](canon://engine/eigen.md) — open, descend, close, lock:

1. **Open the outer loop (the introduction).** The introduction is the command that opens the
   project's loop: motivation, framing, and the **roadmap of descent** — the ordered promise of the
   inner loops the body will close. (Ring I, D-roadmap. Drafted *last*, core-outward, because the
   roadmap can only promise loops that already exist below.)
2. **Descend into the inner loops (the sections).** Each section is an inner loop closing one
   primitive the introduction promised. A section *locks* only when its every claim closes — traces
   to a result at a known status and is grounded with no naked claim. (Ring I + Ring II.)
3. **Bind to the outside corpus (the appendices).** Within sections, the appendices carry the
   granular detail that *binds the work to the corpus of the outside world* — the derivations and
   the deep references that ground the body's primitives in the external substrate. This is the
   form's down-pointer to the literature ([`citation_discipline`](canon://workflow/citation_discipline.md),
   [`pedagogical_segregation`](canon://structure/pedagogical_segregation.md): the algebra goes to
   the appendix). The appendices are where the externally-grounded half of "no naked claims" is
   discharged.
4. **The weave — close to lock, no naked claims (microscopic self-consistency).** Across the whole
   work, self-consistency is maintained by **active checking and referral back** to the conceptric
   substrate, references, and stored results, until the finished product has **no naked claims** —
   every statement grounded in an external reference *or* an internal section/equation pointer
   (Ring II, D-no-naked-claims). The weave is what makes the outer loop actually *lock*: a
   projection with a naked claim is a loop that leaked, and a leaked loop cannot lock — it re-opens
   as the friction worklist.

This is self-similar to the engine's own move (open → descend → close inner loops → lock): the
manuscript is one instance of the loop-as-universal-move, projected into the manuscript medium. The
form object is therefore not a checklist bolted on — it is the *same* architecture the engine runs,
wearing the manuscript's clothes.

## The final stage — the weld (no seam)

The four moves above project the artefact **node-by-node**: the projector walks the form's shells
and each section closes its own loop. But a node can only narrate its **own** role, so each section
announces the trellis it was grown on — "in this section we show…", "we look at X because the
argument requires…", the roadmap of descent re-declared per-section instead of promised once in the
framing rings. This is a meta-instruction with no node-local home: the structure-narrative is
correct, but it leaks into the body as prose *about* the paper. No node can see the whole, so **no
node can dissolve the announcement; only a global pass can.**

The [`welder`](canon://skills/welder.md) is that pass — the **final stage** of the projection loop,
run **once over the whole assembled draft**, after the node-by-node projection is complete and every
form-demand is met, and **before** the artefact is judged. It operates only on the **rhetorical
surface** over a draft that is already form-compliant and content-locked: it confines the
structural / roadmap narrative to the framing rings, replaces every sentence that *announces* a
property with prose that *exhibits* it (the motive moved, never deleted), and smooths the
cross-section seams into one voice, until the artefact is **isomorphic to its core idea** — the
outer ring a seamless external face of the believed core it grew from. It is the completion of
[`object_not_artefact`](canon://workflow/object_not_artefact.md): that law removes leaked *labels*
node-locally; the welder removes the leaked *narrative* that only the whole draft makes visible.

The welder **never touches substance** — not a claim, citation, equation, figure, or epistemic
status; a smoothing that would require changing substance is a typed friction, not an edit. And it
**never certifies its own weld**: the seam is exactly what a cold reader would spy, so the no-seam
test is judged from **outside** by [`blind_reader`](canon://skills/blind_reader.md) — *the point of
closing the loop is that there is no seam through which the construction can be spied.* The welder
owns the detail of all four moves, the guardrail, and the seam test; this node owns only that the
weld is the loop's final stage.

## Clean separation from content (the invariant)

The form object names **no physics**. It says "every literature reliance needs a real citation",
never "cite Witten 1982"; "the apex postulate gate precedes its consequences", never "the apex is
the Loss Hamiltonian". The v7 build-spec mixed both — the form-rules ("postulate-first custody
gate", "three epistemic tiers never laundered", "no phantom panels", "real corpus keys") are
**form** and belong here; the v7 specifics ("`H = -T²∇² + W(θ)`", the shell table, the FORK-A/B
physics) are **content** and stay in the v7 substrate. The test is the
[`object_not_artefact`](canon://workflow/object_not_artefact.md) test: a sentence whose only content
is the rule that generated it is the leak. The form is expressed through the projection's *shape*;
it never appears in the projection's *prose*.

A consequence: this one form object projects *any* manuscript when paired with *any* content
conceptric. The v7 physics letter is one projection; a different content conceptric paired with the
same form projects a different manuscript with the same closed loops.

## Invariants

- **Two conceptrics, one projection.** The projector takes form × content. The form supplies
  demands; the content supplies substance; the friction is where a demand meets a missing substance.
- **The form names no content.** Any physics specific in the form object is a leak — it belongs in
  the content conceptric. Cleanly separable, or it is not the form.
- **No naked claims is the lock condition.** A projection locks only when every claim is grounded —
  external reference or internal pointer. A naked claim is a leaked loop; it re-opens as friction.
- **An unmet demand is a typed friction, never a confabulation.** The shared
  [`generation_discipline`](canon://workflow/generation_discipline.md) §2: every gap carries a
  CONTENT/FORM/TENSION attribution, the projector reports it and does not invent over it, and the
  fix routes to the right conceptric (CONTENT grows the content node and re-projects; FORM grows
  this object; TENSION is surfaced, not silently resolved).
- **Builder ≠ judge.** The shared discipline's §2(c): the projector builds; quality (especially
  clarity) is judged from outside its context. The coherence audit is the projector's internal
  gate, never the verdict on quality.
- **Self-similar to the loop.** The form is open → descend → close inner loops → lock. It is an
  instance of the engine's universal move, not a checklist beside it.
- **Content true, artefact housed.** The first three rings make the content true, grounded, and
  legible; Ring IV makes the artefact the house's (REVTeX/APS class, house figures, house voice,
  Forge compile). A paper can close all of content and still fail the house — a complete paper in
  plain `article` with generic plots is typeset, not projected.
- **Ring IV points, it does not copy.** Each presentation demand carries a `canon://` edge to the
  standard that owns it; the form holds the demand, the standard holds the detail. Wiring the edge
  makes the standard *surface* at the next projection; it does not re-project a standing artefact.
- **Asset existence is judged from outside.** The shared
  [`generation_discipline`](canon://workflow/generation_discipline.md) §1(b): a figure file's
  existence is never trusted from a node's claim; missing assets fall back to prose or an
  out-of-band registered TODO, never a rendered TODO box in the body.
- **The weld is the final stage, and it leaves no seam.** The node-by-node projection leaks the
  per-section structure-narrative no node can dissolve; the [`welder`](canon://skills/welder.md)
  is the one global pass that removes it, surface-only, never touching substance, judged from
  outside by [`blind_reader`](canon://skills/blind_reader.md). A draft from which a cold reader can
  reverse-engineer the construction has not been welded.

*Experimental and coarse by design — this is the v1 form object, distilled from a single real
projection (the v7 manuscript), now carrying its first refinement: Ring IV, the presentation/format
axis, added after the v8 projection came out in plain `article` with generic geometry because the
form pointed at none of the house standards. Refine when a second projection teaches a sharper line
between a form-demand and a content-specific, or a friction type the three attributions do not
cover. Whether this form is GOOD is the author's verdict; the meta-dreamer built and integrated it
and does not self-certify its quality.*

   **REVTeX4-2 / APS, two-column preprint** as the house default. This is *inferred*: the real
   `paper_operatorial_loss/manuscript/main.tex` uses
   `\documentclass[aps,prx,twocolumn,superscriptaddress,nofootinbib]{revtex4-2}`, and
   `figure_standard` assumes APS column widths and "CM serif via usetex for REVTeX" — but no canon
   node states a one-line document-class axiom. If you want the house class/options/geometry pinned
   exactly (PRX vs PRA vs PRL options, `superscriptaddress`, the preprint vs reprint geometry), say
   so and it becomes a named standard rather than an inferred default — ideally as a short axiom in
   `latex_forge` or `compilation_and_release` that Ring IV then points at.
2. **Peer ring vs folded into clarity?** Presentation/format is placed as a **peer** fourth ring,
   not folded into Ring III (clarity). The argument for peer: appearance is orthogonal to whether a
   stranger can follow the words — a paper can be perfectly clear and still un-housed. The argument
   for folding: a reader's first impression of legibility is partly the house format. Encoded as a
   peer ring; reposition if you read it as a clarity sub-shell.
