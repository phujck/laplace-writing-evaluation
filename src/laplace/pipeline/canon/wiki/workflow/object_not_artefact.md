# Object, not artefact — instruction vs meta-instruction

A generation law, named by the author (2026-06-04): *"we very easily mix instruction and meta
instruction, then are annoyed when the meta instruction surfaces in the projection as a self
assertion. We completely missed the logic that we have to teach you how to generate the object we
actually want, not the artefact of it."*

## The two kinds of instruction

- **Object-instruction** - the content of the thing being made. "The claim is X." "Cite this."
  "The section covers Y." It belongs *in* the artefact.
- **Meta-instruction** - how to generate it. "Derive, do not assert." "Use the voice." "Structure
  before minimisation." "One idea per slide." It is about the *generator*, not the content.

They arrive in one stream and feel identical in the moment, so they get mixed.

## The failure mode

If the engine treats its job as **producing the artefact directly**, a meta-instruction has nowhere
to land except inside the artefact - so it surfaces in the projection as a *self-assertion*: the deck
narrating its own design contract, "earn the equations one piece at a time" printed on a card, a
manuscript stating its own method, a sentence whose only content is the rule that generated it. This
is not surface clumsiness. It is a meta-instruction with no correct home, leaking into the output
because the generator had no slot for it.

## The law

Teach the engine to generate the **object that generates the artefact** - the conceptric, the
generating function, the spine - and route meta-instruction into *that*. Then:

- Object-instruction goes into the **conceptric** (the thing).
- Meta-instruction configures the **generator's policy** (the voice, the firewall, the craft, the
  structure, the taste).
- The artefact is the **projection**. Meta is *expressed through* its form - its order, its earned
  simplicity, its voice - and is never *stated in* its content. A meta-instruction that appears as a
  sentence in the output is, by definition, the **leak**, and the leak is the defect.

## Where it already lives, unnamed

This is the general law over moves the engine already makes in pieces:

- The [context firewall](canon://skills/context_firewall.md) at the scale boundary - lift the general
  intuition, scrub the project jargon - is this law between levels.
- [Conceptric-first](canon://domains/talks/projects/explaining_eigen.md) talk-building - build the
  spine that generates the deck, regenerate the projection, never hand-poke the artefact - is this law
  for a talk. (The first talk leaked design-contract language onto its cards; that was the defect this
  names.)
- [Earned simplicity](canon://aesthetics/earned_simplicity.md) - the rule lives in the generator and
  the sentence just lands - is this law for prose.
- The [self](canon://engine/self.md) at the microexpression - taste shapes the word and never
  announces itself - is this law at the smallest scale.

The [generative loop](canon://engine/generative_loop.md) is where it is enforced: when an instruction
arrives, *type it* - is this the object, or the policy that makes the object - and send it to the
right place. Mixing the two and feeding a direct-artefact generator is what makes the meta surface as
a self-assertion.

## The completion - labels locally, narrative globally

This law catches the leak at the scale a single generator can see. But one form of leak survives it:
a manuscript [projected](canon://workflow/manuscript_projection.md) **node-by-node** has each section
narrate the trellis it was grown on - "in this section we show…", the roadmap of descent re-declared
per-section - because a node can only describe its *own* role. That announcement is a meta-instruction
with no node-local home, and no node can dissolve it, because **no node can see the whole**. This law
removes the leaked *label* (the rule printed as a sentence, scrubbed where it sits); the leaked
*narrative* needs a global view to remove. The [`welder`](canon://skills/welder.md) is this law's
**completion**: the one global pass over the assembled draft that confines the structure-narrative to
the framing rings and replaces every announcement with prose that *embodies* the property instead of
*stating* it - the artefact made to *do* the thing rather than *say* it does, surface-only, never
touching substance, judged for *no seam* from outside by
[`blind_reader`](canon://skills/blind_reader.md).
