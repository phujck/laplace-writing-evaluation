keys

type
click into a paragraph — or a plain-text section title — and type; the caret is the edit door. A floating card at the caret names the source lines, takes the altitude (click a chip; Alt+1–6 too; sentence unless set), and says the state. NOTHING banks until your hand says: edits PARK per paragraph (amber edge; the count on the rail; drafts survive a reload). Ctrl-S banks ALL standing paragraphs; Shift-Enter banks this one; Enter splits at the caret (banked at once); Backspace at a paragraph's start / Delete at its end joins (same door); a selection across paragraphs + typing, Ctrl-X, or paste rewrites across them as one row; a multi-paragraph paste makes real paragraphs. Esc reverts the paragraph under the caret; the band lists every park with its delta and its own bank/revert doors. Ctrl-Z is native inside the edit in flight; u revokes banked rows. The receipt lands on the card and in the session band
r
the live redline, toggled: this walk's insertions marked on the face, each deletion struck through beside its replacement, parked deltas included — what stands changed before the next pass. A change that cannot be shown inline wears a loud badge on its paragraph; the clean face is one r away
note
the card's note field: a note alone banks a comment on the paragraph (your selection when one stands, the whole paragraph otherwise); a note beside a typed edit banks ONE compound row — replacement + rationale. The card's dashed asks bank typed requests: refs found for a passage, a figure tagged for remake (click a figure to open its card). "where this sits · links" shows the paragraph's spine position, its refs out, and what references it — each a jump door
click
on a formula, a citation number, a cross-ref, or a macro mark: its source opens in place, the exact span selected. For a citation the note names what the key resolves to
select
selecting text in a block arms the composer with that passage, editable, and the block's source span riding it
Enter
in the composer: bank. The passage edited banks a rewrite; a note alone banks a comment; both bank a compound row. Shift-Enter starts a new line
Alt+1 – 6
set the scope from ANYWHERE — the paper, the passage, the note, a flipped block's source, the band: 1 sentence · 2 structure · 3 voice · 4 artefact · 5 meta · 6 delivery. It never depends on where the caret is
7 · 8 · 9
RULE the passage: 7 keep · 8 cut · 9 rework. The ruling rides the deposit at paragraph, section or chapter altitude — the grain your gesture addressed, unless you name one — and the door compares that passage's own identity before it banks: if it moved while you were reading, the deposit is REFUSED with the current identity rather than banked against text you never saw. Press the same digit again to clear it; a comment with no ruling leaves the passage's fate open, which is what a comment has always done. A session reading this project is told the ruling in words
1 – 6
a bare digit sets the scope only where there is nothing to type into: a scope chip, the shape line or the routing line in the composer (Tab to one), or a row in the session band. Inside any text field a bare digit TYPES — and in the passage that IS the rewrite gesture, so the passage's own label and the shape line say so in the same frame
where each
scope lives
the six altitudes each have a gesture that naturally reaches them, and the frozen rule set leans that way — marked as a suggestion, never pre-selected:
sentence — edit the passage in the composer: the rewrite is the sentence-altitude gesture, and the rewrite door banks it there by default.
structure — span the spine (click a row, shift-click another) and deposit on the span: the spine is the structural surface.
voice — a note on a selection with the passage left alone; register words (sounds, reads, tone, stiff, wordy…) lean it here.
artefact — 0, the whole-paper composer: a verdict on the paper with no source span.
meta — a span deposit carrying apparatus words (claim, evidence, ordering of the argument…), beside the structure a span otherwise is.
delivery — a whole-paper deposit carrying handover words (name, file, pdf, version, deliver, hand…).
None of them needs a new key: Alt+1–6 sets any of the six from anywhere, and the band re-scopes afterwards
