# Glossary

The few distinctions that keep your work and choices clear.

## Writing and reading

Project: related writing, sources, tasks and conversations.

Draft: an authored draft document can already be saved; a workflow local draft has unsaved version changes.

Saved: the app recorded this change. A PDF build or independent judgement is separate.

Source: a document or project file. Linking an existing source is advanced; importing a local document needs no path. Developer Source is application code.

Inspected reading: only the exact window/page opened, not unseen remainder.

Message target: the retained reading/object for the next message; browsing does not replace it.

## Tasks and results

Task (Work): a goal with prerequisites and completion conditions. A programme has its own conditions; one run does not close it.

Ready: current prerequisites allow a next action, not necessarily Done. Waiting: something required is unresolved, not automatically a failed test.

In progress is declared standing. Running needs current observed execution, even after an older Done. No current observation does not mean it never ran.

Review: a judgement on evidence. Required questions need current satisfaction and independence when required; reviewer approval is permission, not that judgement.

Prepared completion: frozen Source, declared proof slots, approved review return. Declared proof arrival is not acceptance. Continue: one owner-checked handoff and review request.

Done with current review needed: the achievement stays recorded while changed scope/evidence needs inspection. Unknown means current proof could not be established, not failure.

Evidence availability: declared supporting items may be present, changed or unavailable; direct completion records can match while old raw is missing, and legacy support may be undeclared.

Activity: recorded events and destinations; old engine work orders/promises are not Tasks or running jobs.

Local accepted, live, distributed: reviewed candidate, running app and published edition. Each needs its own dated record; counts prove no such join.

## Workflows and recovery

Workflow: reusable saved steps. Save, prepare and start are separate.

Run: one attempt on particular inputs/settings, not task acceptance.

Context is delivered content; grouping inherits no transcript or permission and starts no child.

Approval permits bounded future action; it starts nothing.

Uncertain request or Stop: keep and check the original; never resend automatically. Resend original Stop unchanged repeats it once; it is not a new Stop. Stopped: gated, every child settled.

Exact details: owner, reference, basis and receipt identify what was read/changed.

Handover: permitted content copied to a fresh recipient home. Accounts, grants, host proof and unsaved browser state do not travel as authority.
