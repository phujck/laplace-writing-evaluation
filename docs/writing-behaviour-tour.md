# Try the implemented behaviours

Explore a small project with inspectable source, actions and records.

## Open the example

The Behaviour example joins writing, sources, tasks, calculations and their saved records. The example guide also gives the command to generate a fresh workspace.

1. Choose Projects, then Behaviour example (behaviour-example).
2. Open Writing to read and edit its short manuscript. Save a change, use Undo, and reopen it.
3. Open Conversation to inspect the saved reply, actions and proposed edits.
4. Open Tasks to follow the author, review and recorded outcome.
5. Open Research to inspect the calculation, source data, figure and execution records.

- [Examples, commands and behaviour map](behaviours/index.md)

## Follow the evidence

Each example links its implemented owner to the commands, input and resulting records. Current, retained and refused results describe different observed outcomes.

1. Choose a behaviour in the example guide and follow its source and record links.
2. Inspect the original request and outcome before repeating an action.
3. Run a fresh example to compare its positive path and guarded refusal with the retained records.
