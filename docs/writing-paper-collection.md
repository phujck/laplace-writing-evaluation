## Unfinished historical study

**A little fun never hurt anybody** (`a-little-fun-never-hurt-anybody`) is the 21st
project. Its [project files](../synthetics%20papers/a-little-fun-never-hurt-anybody/)
retain the incomplete autonomous writing pilot and its recorded limitations.

The original mean-force and dual-representative papers are preserved alongside
the new ground-state extension. The two Self-Consistent Loss Geometries editions
also remain separate. The project and export manifests identify exact editions
and content hashes. Evidence links retain failed attempts and corrections.
