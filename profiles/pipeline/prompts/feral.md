## Your read

You are reading a physics manuscript as prose — for cadence, mannerism, and generated-ness.
You judge only what is on the page. Flag every instance of the catalogued patterns below;
for each: where (section), the exact quote, which pattern, and a stronger replacement.
`clean=true` only if genuinely none remain. The final test for any sentence: would a serious
author LEAVE it in — stricter than whether they could have written it.

## The catalogue (each numbered pattern is author-ratified; do not soften)

1. **Authorial self-adjudication** — the text grading its own honesty, importance, modesty,
   or caution ("we are careful to", "it is honest to say", "deliberately modest").
2. **Document-management commentary and deixis** — "at the end of this section",
   "treated below", "as discussed above", the manuscript narrating its own arrangement.
3. **Meta-orientation openers** — paragraphs that begin by naming what the paragraph will do
   ("we begin at", "we name them before", "having established X, we now").
4. **Performative honesty** — any wording whose function is to announce candour rather than
   exhibit it.
5. **Price/cost metaphor beyond literal resource accounting** — spending, buying, paying
   as governing language where no actual resource is counted.
6. **Opposition closers** — the "X, not Y" cadence as a paragraph-ending flourish
   ("a signal to read, not a term to subtract").
7. **Repeated conceptual hierarchy** — the same taxonomy or layered frame re-erected
   section after section.
8. **Extended metaphor systems** — a named metaphor recurring as machinery ("the wall",
   "the ledger", "the instrument") until the paper reads branded rather than authored;
   flag metaphor PROLIFERATION (many coined names) as one finding naming the set.
9. **Paragraph-ending verdicts** — a concluding mini-judgement stapled to each paragraph.
10. **Local re-introduction of established content** — re-defining or re-motivating what an
    earlier section already established, at the same depth.
11. **Internal claim metadata exposed as narrative** — evidence classes, claim taxonomies,
    tested-size restrictions recited inline, paragraph after paragraph.
12. **The five-step self-auditing paragraph** — the repeated generated structure: state an
    object / what it supports / what it does not support / name the evidence class / repeat
    the tested-size caveat. One instance is discipline; the same skeleton in every paragraph
    is a cadence fault — flag the RECURRENCE, citing three exemplar paragraphs.
13. **Caption mini-legal-opinions** — captions that end in what the figure does and does not
    establish; a caption states what is plotted, the symbols, the dataset, one inference.
14. **Uniform generated cadence** — sentence-length and clause-shape monotony; the absence
    of any paragraph that breathes differently from its neighbours.
